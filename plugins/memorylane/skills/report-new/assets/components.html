08 · Appendix

Method, assumptions & what would sharpen it

The page your CFO pokes at. Nothing here is decorative.

Reasoning log (7 candidates · 7 processes · 0 dropped)

CandidateVerdictWhy
Lead routingprocess~40 cases/wk, stable 5-step shape, operational.
Pipeline reportprocessWeekly cadence, fixed source set, repeatable.
Slack triagenot a processInterruptions, no stable cross-app workflow.
Deck designnot a processCreative/judgment; logged, excluded from automate.

Assumptions

Saw lead routing ~80× in 14 days; assume ~40/wk steady; so ~2,080/yr.
Loaded rate $62/h (est. from RevOps Analyst, Sydney + ×1.3 overhead). No salary supplied.
Automatable fraction 0.8 for routing = sum of step times tagged automatable.

What would sharpen this

  • Give a real loaded salary → tightens every $ figure.
  • 30–45 day window → confirms monthly processes (board deck).
  • Enable OCR on Salesforce → recover case IDs, lift confidence.
  • Hand-label 1 day → converts durations from reasoned to seen.

How to read the numbers

seen = read straight from data. reasoned = inferred from clear signals (durations are gap-approximated). estimated = role/industry assumption. Deterministic numbers reproduce exactly on a re-run; judgment calls (clustering, common path, automatability) may vary slightly.

Scope: one observed person (n=1), 14 days. Team totals would need headcount and are not shown.

09 · In one breath

The takeaway

This role spends about 22 hours a week on repeat work, and roughly six of those are pure re-keying of inbound leads.

That one job is clean enough to hand to an automation today, with the tools already in place, paying for itself inside two months. Two more, the weekly report and renewal briefs, are strong AI-assist wins where the software drafts and the person decides. Do those three and you give this person back close to half a day every week, worth about $48k a year, without losing the parts of the job that need judgment.
